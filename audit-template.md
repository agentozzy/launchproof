## Findings

### LP-001 — Finding title

- **Priority:** Blocker / Before launch / Planned improvement
- **Boundary:** user journey, data flow, or operational process
- **Evidence:** file and line, failing test, or reproducible local steps
- **Observed behaviour:** what happened, without speculation
- **Likely impact:** who or what may be affected
- **Recommended change:** smallest useful remediation
- **Proof of fix:** exact test or observation required
- **Limitations:** what the review could not establish
